# Example Services Agreement

This fictional agreement is a demonstration fixture, not a legal template.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing thirty days of written notice. A material breach that remains uncured for fifteen days after written notice permits immediate termination.

## Payment

Invoices are payable within forty-five days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at one percent per month. The annual subscription fee is twenty-four thousand dollars.

## Limitation of liability

The aggregate liability of either party shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim. This limitation does not apply to fraud or willful misconduct.
