# Example Annual Report

This fictional report is a demonstration fixture.

## Financial performance

Revenue increased from eighty million dollars to one hundred million dollars, representing twenty-five percent annual growth. Operating expenses were seventy million dollars. Operating income was thirty million dollars.

## Business risks

The company faces foreign exchange risk because forty percent of sales are denominated in foreign currencies. Supplier concentration also poses a risk: a single supplier provides sixty percent of critical components.

## Capital allocation

The company invested twelve million dollars in research and development and repurchased five million dollars of common stock.
