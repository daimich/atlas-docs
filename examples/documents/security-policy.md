# Example Security Policy

This fictional policy is a demonstration fixture.

## Authentication

All employees must use multifactor authentication for administrative access. Access reviews occur quarterly. Sessions expire after thirty minutes of inactivity.

## Incident response

Critical security incidents must be reported to the incident response team within four hours of discovery. The incident commander coordinates containment and publishes a postmortem within five business days.

## Retention

Audit logs are retained for three hundred sixty-five days. Customer data is deleted within sixty days after termination, except where retention is required by law.
